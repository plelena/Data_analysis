**QuadWalk** is a global footwear brand operating in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Germany.  
This interactive **marketing dashboard** in Tableau consolidates sales, discounts, and product performance data, enabling the marketing team to monitor KPIs, identify trends, and optimize promotional strategies in real time.

---

## 📌 Business Case

### Company Background
QuadWalk is a mid-sized international footwear retailer specializing in stylish and comfortable shoes for men and women.  
The company manages both physical stores and an online presence, selling across four countries with a diverse customer base and seasonal demand patterns.

### Problem Statement
Before this dashboard was introduced:
- **Data silos** existed between countries — sales and marketing teams worked in isolation.
- **Promotion performance** was difficult to evaluate quickly, leading to inefficient budget allocation.
- Reports were generated manually from spreadsheets, which was **time-consuming** and prone to errors.
- Managers lacked a **single, clear view** of sales trends, discount efficiency, and product performance across all markets.

### Objectives
1. **Unify sales and marketing data** into a single visualization tool.
2. **Track campaign effectiveness** by analyzing sales volumes, revenue, and discount patterns.
3. **Identify top-performing stores and products** to replicate success strategies.
4. **Enable real-time decision-making** for promotions, inventory planning, and pricing.

### Implemented Solution
The Marketing Dashboard was designed to provide:
- **KPI Overview:** Total sales, discount share, average order value, and units sold.
- **Top 10 Stores:** Ranked by sales volume with benchmark comparison against the global average.
- **Sales Trends:** Monthly performance charts with the ability to filter by country, gender, and store.
- **Discount Analysis:** Visualization of discounted vs. full-price sales to evaluate promotion impact.
- **Customer Preferences:** Breakdown of sales by gender and popular shoe sizes for targeted marketing.

### Business Impact
- Reduced campaign performance analysis time from **4–5 hours** to just a few minutes.
- Increased transparency across marketing, retail, and management teams.
- Supported **data-driven decision-making** on pricing and promotional planning.
- Improved marketing ROI by focusing on the most effective campaigns and customer segments.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Data Sources:** POS system exports, CRM data
- **Tools:** Tableau Public, Excel
- **Techniques:** FIXED LOD expressions, custom date filters, dynamic KPI calculations, dual-axis charts
